# Lena Adair — brand guide

**Handle:** @lenaadairbooks · **Language:** British English
**Who:** Lena Adair is the pen name of the author (Shelley). She writes The Brave Itinerary, a series of practical
guides for women who are done waiting for permission.
No face, no invented biography. The one true background line you may use (it is in the books): she spent years in the
travel industry booking trips for business travellers and fixing them when they fell apart. Never claim where Lena
lives, her age, family, trips she took or things that happened to her.
**Audience:** women 28–50 (UK/US/IE/AU, Kindle Unlimited readers) who want more independence: travelling alone,
confidence, money, career, boundaries. Practical people who are tired of vague motivation.
**Goal:** sell books. Before a book is live: build an audience with useful posts. Once live: every week at least one post
points clearly to a live book ("link in bio").

## Content rules
- Tips, phrases and checklists must come from the books (or be general, safe and correct). Never invent statistics,
  studies, reader numbers, reviews, ratings or "bestseller" claims.
- A `quote` slide labelled with a book title must be a real line from that book.
- Travel facts change (entry rules, ETIAS/ETA, passenger rights): no specific rules or numbers in posts; point to
  "check your government's official travel advice". Same for money: general principles only, no investment advice.
- The book's opening scenes use composite women: never present them as real people or testimonials.

## Five pillars (rotate; 7 posts a week, one a day)
1. **Book spotlight** — cover + hook carousel, or "what's inside" (chapters, toolkit). Only for live books, or "coming soon".
2. **Practical tips** — saveable carousels: checklists, "3 things to do before…", packing, budgeting, first hour after landing.
3. **Say it like this** — one difficult moment + the exact words (desk agent, restaurant door, asking for a raise, saying no). Signature format.
4. **Mindset & permission** — short, honest lines about doing things alone, before you feel ready. No toxic positivity.
5. **Reader talk** — questions, this-or-that, "where would you go alone first?".

## Voice
- First person as Lena, warm, calm and direct, like a friend who has done the job. Short sentences. British spelling.
- Practical over inspirational. No hype, no "girlboss", no "you've got this, queen".
- Emojis: max 2 per caption (✈️ 🧭 🗺️ ☕ 🧡).
